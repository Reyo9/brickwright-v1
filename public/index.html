How it works

From idea to Studio in three steps

1

Describe it

Write what you want — proportions, colors, the details that matter. Pick Quick, Standard or Detailed.

2

Watch it build

The AI writes the script, our checker runs it and fixes floating parts or broken colors for free, and you spin the result in 3D.

3

Paste into Studio

Copy the script into Studio’s command bar and press Enter. The model appears in Workspace, fully studded.
