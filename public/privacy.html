Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This explains what Brickwright collects, why, and what you can do about it. We keep it to what the service needs.

What we collect

  • Your email address — to sign you in with a code and to identify your account.
  • What you create — your prompts, reference images you upload, generated scripts, build history, rigs and animations.
  • Credits and plan — how many credits you’ve used, your plan, and (if you subscribe) a Stripe customer ID. We never see or store your card details; Stripe handles them.
  • Abuse prevention — your IP address and a random device ID stored in your browser, used only to limit fake accounts and protect the free credits.
  • Basic usage counts — daily totals like number of builds and sign-ups, so we can keep the service running well.

Services that process data for us

  • Netlify — hosts the website and runs our server code.
  • Upstash — stores accounts, credits, builds and history.
  • Google (Gemini API) — receives your prompt and any reference image to generate the script.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile — the “are you human” check on sign-in.
  • Gmail / email provider — sends your login codes.
  • Stripe — processes payments if you buy a plan or credits.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell your personal data.
  • We don’t use advertising trackers.
  • Builds are private unless you choose to share them to Explore. Shared builds show the creator name you type when sharing — never your email.

How long we keep it

Build history is kept for up to about 4 months (your newest 40 builds). Login codes expire after 10 minutes. Abuse-prevention records expire after 30 days to about a year. Shared builds stay until you or we remove them.

Cookies and browser storage

We use your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in and to remember a device ID. Cloudflare Turnstile may set its own cookies to run the human check. We don’t use advertising cookies.

Your choices

You can delete builds from your history and remove builds you’ve shared. To get a copy of your data or delete your account, reply to any Brickwright login email and we’ll handle it.

Children

Brickwright is a creative tool for Roblox builders. If you’re under 18, use it with a parent or guardian’s permission. If you think a child has given us personal data without permission, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

We’ll update the date at the top when this policy changes.
